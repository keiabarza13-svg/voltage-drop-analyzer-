What is Voltage Drop?
In electrical engineering, voltage drop is the decrease of electrical potential along the path of a current flowing in an electrical circuit.

Excessive voltage drop can cause motors to run hot, lights to flicker, and electronic equipment to malfunction. This tool ensures your conductor sizes comply with Philippine Electrical Code (PEC) standards by considering temperature effects, material properties, and installation methods.
